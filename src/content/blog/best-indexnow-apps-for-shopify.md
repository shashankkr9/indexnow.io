Shopify does not support the IndexNow protocol natively — you cannot host the required key verification file at your domain root, and there is no built-in way to ping search engines when a product changes. So if you want your store's new and updated URLs pushed to Bing and Yandex the moment they change, you need an app.

The good news: there are several solid IndexNow apps on the Shopify App Store. The better news: at least one of them is completely free. This post compares the main options honestly — including where each one is strong — so you can pick in five minutes and get back to running your store.

If you are not yet sure *why* you would want IndexNow at all, the short version: Bing's crawler visits smaller stores infrequently, so new products can take weeks to appear in Bing — and Bing's index also powers DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, ChatGPT search, and Copilot. IndexNow tells those engines about URL changes instantly instead of waiting to be crawled.

Who it is for: stores selling across multiple markets and languages, or merchants who want detailed submission logs and are happy to pay a modest subscription (or a one-time $70) for them. The 5.0★ rating, while based on a smaller review count, suggests users who choose it are satisfied.

## How to choose

A quick decision framework:

**Start with free unless you have a reason not to.** IndexNow is a simple protocol — a ping saying "this URL changed." Every app in this list sends the same ping to the same endpoint. The differentiation is in the surrounding tooling, not in the pings themselves. If auto-submission plus bulk submission is all you need (and for most stores it is), [Bing SEO: IndexNow for Bing](https://apps.shopify.com/bing-seo-indexnow-for-bing) covers it at no cost.

**Pay if you need multi-market support or detailed logs.** Running Shopify Markets with localized storefronts in several languages? StoreSpark's multi-language/market handling is the concrete feature that justifies its price. Similarly, if you want a proper submission audit trail for debugging or reporting, its indexing logs are the most developed in this group.

**Consider the AI-feed bundle if you are experimenting with LLM visibility.** If you specifically want LLMs.txt or an AI product feed and would rather not run a separate app for it, IndexNow: Indexing & LLMs.txt is the combined option. Just go in knowing the AI-feed side is a bet on an emerging convention, not a proven traffic channel.

**Don't overweight star ratings at these sample sizes.** 5.0★ over 15 reviews versus 4.6★ over 29 reviews are both small samples and both healthy signals. Read a few recent reviews on each listing rather than deciding on the decimal.

Whichever app you pick, do the same two things after installing: run a **bulk submission** of your existing URLs (automatic pings only cover future changes), and set up [Bing Webmaster Tools](/blog/bing-webmaster-tools-for-shopify/) so you can actually watch your indexed-page count move. If your store has deeper indexing problems, an app alone will not fix them — see [why your Shopify store isn't showing up on Bing](/blog/shopify-store-not-showing-up-on-bing/) and [Bing "discovered but not crawled"](/blog/bing-discovered-but-not-crawled/) first.

## What IndexNow won't do

Worth being clear-eyed about, no matter which app you install:

- **It speeds up indexing, not rankings.** IndexNow gets your URLs *seen* by Bing and Yandex faster. Where those pages rank still depends on your content, site quality, and overall [Bing SEO](/blog/bing-seo-for-shopify/). No app can promise rankings, and you should be skeptical of any that does.
- **Google is unaffected.** Google does not participate in the IndexNow protocol. Nothing in this comparison changes your Google indexing or rankings — keep your sitemap submitted to Google Search Console for that.
- **It is not a substitute for the basics.** A submitted URL that is blocked by robots rules, thin, or duplicated still will not index well. IndexNow removes the *discovery* delay; the rest of SEO still applies.

Within those limits, it is one of the highest-leverage low-effort wins in Shopify Bing SEO: a one-time install that permanently removes the wait between "I published this" and "Bing knows about it."

## FAQ

### Do I really need an app for IndexNow on Shopify?

Effectively, yes. IndexNow requires hosting a key verification file at your domain root, which Shopify does not allow, and something must send a ping on every product, collection, page, or blog change. An app handles both. The only app-free alternative is manually submitting URLs through Bing Webmaster Tools, which does not scale.

### Can I run more than one IndexNow app at once?

There is no benefit. IndexNow deduplicates submissions, and one ping per changed URL is all any search engine needs. Running multiple apps just duplicates webhook processing. Pick one.

### Which app is best for ChatGPT visibility?

They are all equivalent on this point. ChatGPT search draws on Bing's index, so any app that submits your URLs via IndexNow helps the same way — by getting your pages into Bing faster. Apps that market ChatGPT indexing specifically are describing this same mechanism.

### Does a higher star rating mean a better app here?

At these sample sizes (15–29 reviews), treat 4.6★ and 5.0★ as roughly equivalent quality signals. Read recent reviews for specifics — reliability of auto-submission and quality of support matter more than the decimal point.

### Will switching apps later cause problems?

No. IndexNow keys are cheap to generate and verify, and submissions are stateless from your store's perspective. Uninstall one app, install another, re-run a bulk submission, and you are fully migrated.
